Built for respectful first impressions

Matrimonial biodata is often the first formal introduction. We believe it should be clear, culturally aware, and easy for every generation to read.

Our story

Vivah Biodata began as a small project within a design studio that saw the same frustration again and again: families spending hours formatting Word documents, only to end up with inconsistent spacing and fonts that looked tired on mobile screens.

We partnered with elders, matchmakers, and young professionals to shape section order, language tone, and print margins that feel familiar in Indian contexts — without slipping into clutter.

What we care about

  • Dignity — your profile is personal; the design should support that.
  • Clarity — information hierarchy that parents and relatives can scan quickly.
  • Speed — a calm workflow from template to PDF in one sitting.

Talk to us if you represent a community organization and want template guidance at scale.
